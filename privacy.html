Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Renta.ph is a free marketplace in the Philippines that connects people who need a service, a vehicle, a place to rent or a product with the local people who offer them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you sign in with Google or an email and password.
  • Provider profile: the business or display name and mobile number you add before posting a listing.
  • Listings: titles, categories, prices, location (province, city or municipality and barangay), descriptions and up to 6 photos you upload.
  • Requests and messages: the dates, quantities, address, messages and mobile number you include when you send a request, and the chat messages that follow.
  • Map pin (optional): if you pin your listing’s location, we save that spot, or only the approximate area if you choose that. If you tap “Use my current location”, your phone’s location is used once to place the pin and isn’t stored anywhere else.
  • Ratings: the stars and comment you leave for a provider after a completed request.
  • Saved listings and alert settings: the listings you save, and a device code that lets us send you alerts if you turn them on.

Apart from an optional map pin, we don’t collect your location, and we never collect your contacts or payment details. We don’t show ads.

How we use it

  • To show listings, provider names, numbers and ratings to people browsing Renta.ph.
  • To deliver your requests and messages to the other person, and to alert you about new ones.
  • To keep the service safe, for example by removing listings that break our terms.

Who can see what

  • Public: listings, photos, map pins, provider names and numbers, and ratings with the reviewer’s name.
  • Only the two people in a request: the request details, your address, your mobile number and your chat messages.
  • Only you: your saved listings and alert settings.

We don’t sell your information. Renta.ph runs on Google Firebase, which stores the data on Google’s servers and handles sign-in and alerts on our behalf.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can edit or delete any listing at any time from My listings.

Delete your account
In the app, tap your profile icon (top right), then Delete my account. This removes your listings, photos, provider profile, saved listings and sign-in. Requests and chat messages you already exchanged stay visible to the other person in that conversation, and ratings you left may remain. To have those removed too, or if you can’t sign in, contact us at the support email shown on our Google Play page.

Children

Renta.ph is meant for people 18 and older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. Using Renta.ph after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: the support email shown on our Google Play page.
