About Renta.ph

Free marketplace for local services, rentals and stores across the Philippines

Renta.ph helps you find the people near you who fix, clean, rent and sell, and helps them get found.

Book a plumber or a house cleaner, rent a car, motorbike or room, or order from a home-based kitchen. Every listing shows the price, the location and how to reach the person behind it. You deal with them directly: no middleman, no commission.

Our purpose: support local businesses

Small providers keep neighborhoods running, but many depend on word of mouth. Renta.ph gives them a free place to be found.

  • Free to post, with no commission, so providers keep 100% of what they earn.
  • Built for the plumber, the cleaner, the landlord, the car and motor owner, and the sari-sari store down the street.
  • Honest ratings from real completed requests help good providers stand out.
